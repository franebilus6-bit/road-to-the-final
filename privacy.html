Privacy policy

Road to the Final · last updated 20 November 2026

Road to the Final is a free daily football quiz made by Frane Biluš for fun. This page explains what data the game keeps.

Signing in with Google

If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID and email address through our login provider, Supabase. We use them only to recognise you when you come back. We never post anything to your Google account and never see your Google password.

What we store

  • The display name you choose, which other players can see on the leaderboards.
  • Your daily results: kilometres, number of correct answers and the answers you gave.
  • The leagues you create or join.

Your browser also keeps your recent results and settings in local storage so the game works without signing in.

Who can see it

Your display name and results are shown on the public leaderboard and in the leagues you are a member of. Your email address is never shown to other players and is not shared with or sold to anyone.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (servers in Frankfurt, EU). The website is hosted by Netlify.

Deleting your data

Email frane.bilus6@gmail.com and your account, name and results will be deleted.
