You get 5 seconds to read each question and 25 to answer. A wrong answer or running out of time scores 0 km. The road ends at the final in Madrid (3,400 km); every kilometre past it is bonus that carries you on to Benfica's Estádio da Luz in Lisbon. Rarity comes from real English Wikipedia page views over the last five years (Nov 2021 to Oct 2026): 40% from the square root of views, 60% from simulated players who answer in proportion to views. Rocket limit: only 4 SIUUU launches count each day; every SIUUU after the fourth crashes you back to 0 km.
